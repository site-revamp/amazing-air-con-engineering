What aircon services does Amazing Aircon provide?

Amazing Aircon lists general servicing, repair, maintenance, installation, chemical overhaul and chemical wash services for residential and commercial customers.

Can I arrange aircon repair for a leaking unit?

Yes. The company lists troubleshooting and repair for common issues including water leaks, weak cooling, unusual noises and system faults. Contact the team to describe your symptoms.

Do you provide aircon installation?

Yes. Amazing Aircon provides installation support for HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes, offices and commercial properties.

Do you serve both homes and businesses?

Yes. The company states that it supports homeowners, tenants, landlords, offices, retail shops and other commercial customers.

How can I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp the team with your location, the number of units and the service you need. The team can advise on the next step and quotation.

How often should I service my aircon?

It depends on usage, the environment and the condition of your system. Regular servicing helps keep filters and components clean and can help identify potential issues earlier.
